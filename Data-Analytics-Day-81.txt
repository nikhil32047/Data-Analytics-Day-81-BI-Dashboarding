 Business Intelligence & Dashboarding (Power BI + Tableau)

🔹 Lesson 1: Advanced Power BI – DAX & Automation
===================================================
Summary:
Data Analysis Expressions (DAX) bring intelligence into your Power BI models.
Key functions: CALCULATE(), FILTER(), SUMX(), RELATED()
Create custom KPIs and measures
Automate data refreshes with Power BI Service

Example:
In a sales dashboard, create a KPI to show “YoY Growth = (CurrentYearSales – PreviousYearSales) / PreviousYearSales” using DAX.

Video: Master DAX in Power BI (25 mins)


🔹 Lesson 2: Tableau Storytelling & Calculated Fields
=======================================================
Summary:
Tableau helps you narrate insights visually.
Build calculated fields for dynamic metrics
Use Story Points to walk users through insights
Combine text, visuals, and interactivity for storytelling

Example:
A Tableau story for a retail company: “From declining profits → to identifying low-margin products → to recommending supplier renegotiation.”

Video: Tableau Storytelling Techniques (18 mins)


🔹 Lesson 3: Data Blending & Cross-Source Insights
=====================================================
Summary:
Often, data is scattered across sources. Tableau and Power BI both support data blending.
Combine Excel, SQL Server, and Google Sheets in one view
Blend by shared dimensions (e.g., Region, Product ID)
Handle mismatched granularity using relationships

Example:
Blend Google Ads spend (Excel) with sales revenue (SQL) to show “Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)” by campaign.

Video: Data Blending in Tableau & Power BI (20 mins)


🔹 Lesson 4: Interactive Dashboards for Decision Makers
==========================================================
Summary:
An effective dashboard provides clarity, not clutter.
Use filters, slicers, bookmarks for user-driven exploration
Apply color psychology (green = growth, red = decline)
Use KPIs, trends, and drill-downs

Example:
Build a Sales Performance Dashboard with filters for region, product category, and salesperson — all interactive and auto-updating.

Video: Create Interactive Dashboards in Power BI (22 mins)

==============================================================

Goal: A company wants one unified dashboard for CEO-level insights.
Steps:
